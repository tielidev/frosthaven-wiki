## Evidence boundaries

This is not a full digital Codex extraction. The official 1.0 announcement confirms all seventeen heroes, but does not by itself verify every tabletop mechanic, level-up choice or unlock requirement. Class strategy is original editorial analysis informed by tabletop mechanics; do not label it playtested or an optimized 1.0 card list. Individual records include evidenceStatus to preserve that distinction in published pages.

Scenario records establish identity only. They must not become invented full walkthroughs. Use them in a clearly labelled partial index or keep incomplete detail pages in draft. Item names do not establish recipes, slots or numeric effects. Enemy spotlights establish broad tactics, not level-based statistics. Some buildings have a historical announced upgrade ceiling; none have verified current costs or requirements in this dataset.
