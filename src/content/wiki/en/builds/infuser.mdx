## What is this build trying to do?

Build around a compact set of mutually useful infusions and reliable element generation. Add movement and ordinary attacks that remain useful when an element is unavailable. Choose whether you are supplying your own engine or sharing with an ally.

This is a role-based strategy framework. It does not claim to be a verified level-by-level digital card, equipment or perk list. Check the current digital card text before making an irreversible selection.

## How do you set up a repeatable turn?

1. Reserve a real element source for the desired infusion.
2. Play the infusion when its immediate effect and persistent benefit both contribute.
3. Continue with ordinary useful actions until another worthwhile infusion window appears.

Agree on element supply with the party before you select an infusion-heavy hand. Prepare the element, then spend it on a turn when the persistent benefit and immediate effect both matter. Avoid treating setup as free damage.

## Which cards should you prioritize?

Pick a compact infusion package, enough element generation and at least one useful non-infusion action. Rising Momentum and Slashing Cyclone are confirmed digital options to inspect for movement-related sequencing. If selecting Imbue with Life, plan around the summon and the persistent disarm it imposes; a patch explicitly prevents removing that disarm through Cure Poison. Gale Barrage is a confirmed attack candidate, not an infinite attack engine.

Compare a candidate card against the job your current hand cannot accomplish: reaching a useful hex, making the primary attack legal, surviving the next enemy action or recovering a disrupted turn. Avoid upgrading a number while losing the only action that keeps the sequence reliable. Exact level selections remain subject to digital Codex verification.

## How should equipment and perks support the plan?

Look for equipment that fills a missing element or supports the repeated action your infusions improve. Check exact consumption timing before committing. Ember Energy Source appears with Gale Barrage in a fixed infinite-attack interaction; use its current legitimate effect, not the historical exploit.

In Tune and Primed are named in digital notes; Primed is documented as improving damage with multiple infusions. Compare that payoff with how many infusions you can afford to maintain and still have a functional hand. Do not prioritize an engine perk before securing its element supply.

## What should you change when the plan fails?

Infusions are not costless extra turns. Imbue with Life can fundamentally change what the character can do, and canceling its persistent effect changes that state. A general healer cannot necessarily remove a condition intentionally maintained by an active class card.

Identify whether the failure was positioning, timing, a resource shortage or a party mismatch before replacing the entire hand. Record a change and test it over another expedition rather than changing several decisions at once.
