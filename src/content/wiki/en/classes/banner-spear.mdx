## What does Banner Spear bring to a party?

**Role:** Formation melee / support. **Core idea:** Uses allies to create formation attacks.

Keep a dependable ally near your intended attack position. Explain the formation you need before a teammate commits to movement, then preserve an escape hex after attacking. A flexible movement card is often worth more than a stronger attack that cannot be positioned.

## How do you unlock this class?

Available when creating a starting party.

## How should you build around its strengths?

Choose movement and formation flexibility first; add healing or banners only when the party can afford the setup. Plan attacks around allies' likely final positions, rather than asking everybody to rebuild their turn around yours.

1. Identify the ally who can stay near the next formation target.
2. Move into a position that completes the required pattern without blocking the passage.
3. Use the formation, then preserve an alternative attack or movement for the next round.

## What commonly goes wrong?

A banner is an ally for some interactions but also occupies a tactically important position. A plan that needs several adjacent banners can congest the doorway and leave the spear or a teammate unable to attack.
