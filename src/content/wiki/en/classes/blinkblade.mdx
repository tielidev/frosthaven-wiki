## What does Blinkblade bring to a party?

**Role:** Mobile melee / burst. **Core idea:** Alternates slow preparation and fast expenditure using time tokens.

Treat a quiet round as preparation for the next engagement. Enter danger late when enemies have already acted, then use a fast turn to attack and leave. The strongest sequence is one you can afford twice, rather than a spectacular opener with no safe follow-up.

## How do you unlock this class?

Available when creating a starting party.

## How should you build around its strengths?

Favor a useful slow action alongside each fast attack plan. Select mobility that ends in cover, and use fast rounds when an early kill prevents an enemy turn. Reserve an exit route before committing to several attacks.

1. Choose a slow round where preparation will not leave an ally unsupported.
2. Spend the resulting tempo on a fast turn that removes a threat before it acts.
3. Move out of the remaining threat zone and check the next slow turn before ending.
