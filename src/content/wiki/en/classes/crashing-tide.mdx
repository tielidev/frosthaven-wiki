## What does Crashing Tide bring to a party?

**Role:** Melee engine / water. **Core idea:** Builds a persistent tide engine and uses water-related effects.

Look for a sustainable engagement where your setup can pay off over several rounds. Coordinate the front line so you are not repeatedly forced to abandon the space your engine supports. Treat water placement as a team movement decision.

## How do you unlock this class?

Campaign unlock; exact digital requirements not yet independently checked.

## How should you build around its strengths?

Choose a focused set of tides that strengthens your most common actions. Add reliable movement before selecting another setup card. When possible, establish the engine near the fight rather than several turns behind it.

1. Select a limited tide package that rewards the actions you expect to repeat.
2. Build the engine close enough to contribute to the current fight.
3. Keep water and movement choices compatible with allies crossing the same area.

## What commonly goes wrong?

Water created by Coral can receive scenario-specific treatment. Official notes correct such an interaction in Rising Brine. Read the current special rules before using self-created water as though it were a universal protection against scenario damage.
