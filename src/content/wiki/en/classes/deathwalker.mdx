## What does Deathwalker bring to a party?

**Role:** Shadow-based damage. **Core idea:** Creates and uses shadows to project attacks and reposition.

Establish your shadow supply before the party runs far ahead. Move the network toward the next fight while keeping your character outside unnecessary threat. Distinguish actions that create shadows from those that merely consume or reposition them.

## How do you unlock this class?

Available when creating a starting party.

## How should you build around its strengths?

Favor shadow generation, movement of shadows and repeatable ranged attacks from useful positions. Keep personal repositioning available so the character can catch up when the encounter moves. Spend shadows when the resulting removal prevents meaningful danger.

1. Establish a way to generate shadows before loading the hand with consumers.
2. Advance the shadow positions toward the party's next target.
3. Attack through the network and reposition the character before it falls too far behind.

## What commonly goes wrong?

Do not spend the same shadow twice in a sequence. Official digital fixes specifically restrict Strength of the Abyss interactions with attacks performed from multiple shadows; inspect the legal selection instead of assuming every displayed shadow is expendable.
