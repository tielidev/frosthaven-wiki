## What does Geminate bring to a party?

**Role:** Dual-form flexible damage. **Core idea:** Switches between distinct melee and ranged forms.

Plan the current turn and the form you will need on the following turn. Count usable cards in each form before resting. The goal is to leave a coherent sequence available, with movement that makes the next form's attacks practical.

## How do you unlock this class?

Available when creating a starting party.

## How should you build around its strengths?

Keep a functional blend of attacks, movement and form-changing options across both forms. Spend a loss when it advances the objective or resolves a dangerous engagement, then rebalance future choices around the reduced hand.

1. Count the currently usable cards in both forms.
2. Choose the next form based on reachable targets and remaining movement.
3. Execute a form transition that leaves a complete pair available for the following round.

## What commonly goes wrong?

A defense card used only for movement can be productive; an element-consuming action canceled without performing its ability is not free engine progress. Check that the intended action resolves before counting its form or element consequences.
