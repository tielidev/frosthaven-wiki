## What does Pyroclast bring to a party?

**Role:** Hazardous-terrain control. **Core idea:** Shapes fights with hazardous terrain.

Place terrain where enemies must make a useful choice, while preserving friendly access to objectives. Plan placement together with any push or pull. Compare the board after the attack with the board after the forced movement before choosing the order.

## How do you unlock this class?

Campaign unlock; exact digital requirements not yet independently checked.

## How should you build around its strengths?

Favor terrain creation, repeatable control and movement that keeps your own character relevant. Build damage through useful paths rather than simply covering empty spaces. Keep alternate answers for flying enemies and encounters that move quickly.

1. Preserve a safe friendly route to the objective.
2. Place terrain where enemy movement and your forced movement can make use of it.
3. Apply the control sequence and reassess how the surviving enemies will travel.
