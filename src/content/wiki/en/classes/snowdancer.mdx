## What does Snowdancer bring to a party?

**Role:** Support / forced movement. **Core idea:** Uses healing, wind and ice with repositioning.

Treat movement of allies and enemies as part of the same puzzle. Clear access for a melee ally, move a threat away from a fragile character, or prepare a hazard interaction. Decide which outcome matters before spending your support action.

## How do you unlock this class?

Campaign unlock; exact digital requirements not yet independently checked.

## How should you build around its strengths?

Bring practical healing and forced movement, with enough movement of your own to stay in range. Pick upgrades for the party's recurring problems. In a melee-heavy group, prioritize effects that help allies reach productive attack positions.

1. Choose whether the round needs healing, enemy displacement or allied access.
2. Check all legal destinations and hazard interactions before selecting forced movement.
3. End within support range of the next fight rather than remaining behind the party.

## What commonly goes wrong?

Gathering Force's effect is corrected not to reduce allied movement. That does not make every forced-movement or movement-granting ability legal on every special object; the Ice Block and other scenario entities have their own restrictions.
