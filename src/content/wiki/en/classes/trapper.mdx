## What does Trapper bring to a party?

**Role:** Trap-based controller. **Core idea:** Creates and manipulates traps.

Work backward from the hex where the target must end up. A powerful trap is only useful if the enemy can be made to trigger it or another ability can apply it. Leave a friendly route through the room and identify flying targets early.

## How do you unlock this class?

Campaign unlock; exact digital requirements not yet independently checked.

## How should you build around its strengths?

Combine trap creation with reliable ways to apply or trigger traps. Preserve movement that crosses your own setup safely when needed. Before upgrading raw trap power, solve any recurring difficulty delivering it to targets.

1. Identify the intended target and a legal path for trap delivery.
2. Build the trap without closing the only safe route for teammates.
3. Trigger or apply the trap through a supported effect rather than hoping for voluntary enemy movement.

## What commonly goes wrong?

A forced-movement chain can expose the Trapper to retaliation. Official Path of Pain fixes explicitly preserve retaliate damage in relevant range. Do not calculate the turn solely from trap damage while ignoring the character's position.
