## What should you read before taking a turn?

Inspect the revealed action, movement, attack range and any special effect. A familiar model does not guarantee a familiar turn. If an enemy is not attacking this round, standing closer may be safe; if it has an unexpected area effect, a previously good formation may become expensive.

## Which enemies are documented in digital sources?

### Abael Herder

![Abael Herder official digital spotlight](/images/entities/abael-herder.webp)

Commands and summons Piranha Pigs, amplifying the group's pressure. Compare killing the herder with thinning its flock; the right target depends on the revealed command and the pigs already active.

![Abael Scout official digital spotlight](/images/entities/abael-scout.webp)

## How do you avoid expensive trades?

Consider the enemy turn that your attack prevents. A kill or effective control action can preserve both hit points and cards. Check whether summons or allies will occupy the hex you need before committing.

Use [scenario references](/scenarios/), compare [class tools](/classes/), and preserve [card economy](/mechanics/card-economy/) when deciding whether to spend a powerful action.
