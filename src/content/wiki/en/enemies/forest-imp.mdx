## What does Forest Imp do?

Small flying curse user.

## How should your party respond?

Do not waste multiple weak attacks if they cannot reliably connect; plan a coordinated removal before curse pressure accumulates.

Inspect the revealed action before selecting your final movement. Decide whether your contribution removes the threat now, prevents its next attack, or leaves an ally to absorb it. The value of a control or defensive action depends on the action this enemy is actually about to perform, not only its health.

## Which statistics should you verify in game?

Check the scenario level, normal or elite status, current health, movement and any printed special properties in the digital interface. This reference does not substitute a fixed tabletop stat block for the current digital enemy. A historical patch note is evidence that a change was announced, not a guarantee that a particular edge case is resolved in every present situation.

## How do you prepare a reliable response?

Choose a position that preserves a second useful action if your intended target dies before your initiative. Coordinate limited space with summons and other melee heroes. Avoid spending a permanent loss solely to increase damage when the same objective can be reached through ordinary actions and safer positioning.
