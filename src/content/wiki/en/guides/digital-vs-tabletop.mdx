## What is shared between the editions?

The digital game adapts the Frosthaven campaign and action-card combat. The same class names and broad concepts make tabletop discussions useful context. That relationship does not establish that every card value, interface option, event sequence or edge case is identical.

## What needs a digital source?

Check the current Codex and game tooltips for card wording, building functions, item effects and scenario special rules. Official 1.0 notes mention a populated Codex, retraining, revised outpost screens and improved scenario rule descriptions. Treat an older bug report as historical until its current status is confirmed.

## How does this wiki distinguish evidence?

Each article records its sources. Tabletop references are labeled as context, and build advice is presented as editorial strategy rather than claimed playtesting. Start with [the beginner guide](/guides/getting-started/), compare [classes](/classes/), or read [1.0 changes](/news/digital-1-0-changes/).
