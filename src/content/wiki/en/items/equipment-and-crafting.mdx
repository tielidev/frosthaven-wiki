## Which equipment problem should you solve first?

Choose equipment to solve a problem that occurred in your last expedition. Movement can make an otherwise unusable turn legal; defensive tools can preserve cards that would be spent preventing damage. Check slot restrictions and current text in the digital Codex before purchasing or crafting.

## How should you prioritize crafting?

Compare material costs with the immediate improvement to your actual hand. Do not craft an item because a tabletop list gives it a high rating without checking the digital effect and availability. Review [your class](/classes/), check [outpost planning](/outpost/outpost-planning/), and match the choice to your [build](/builds/).
