## How do you explore the map?

Open the [interactive world map](/maps/). Drag to move, pinch or use the zoom controls, and reset to return to the whole image. Select the Frosthaven settlement marker to connect the map to outpost guidance. On a phone, switch between map and searchable locations.

## What do the markers mean?

Markers are based on locations identifiable in the official digital screenshot. Unverified scenario positions are deliberately not plotted. A quest being unlocked after another quest does not establish that it is geographically beside that quest.

## How do you find a scenario?

Use the [scenario name-and-number index](/scenarios/scenario-index/), then read the current in-game objective and rules. The wiki does not currently claim a complete coordinate set or full tactical map library. Prepare the party with [getting started](/guides/getting-started/) and [outpost planning](/outpost/outpost-planning/).
