## What launched on October 15?

The full digital release brings together the Early Access journey and the remaining campaign, including its final chapter. The official announcement confirms all 17 playable heroes. Six are available at the start; the others are unlocked as the campaign progresses.

## What should returning players inspect?

Read the populated Codex, scenario special rules and updated outpost interface. Official notes also describe hero retraining and support for several heroes of the same class in the roster, while still excluding duplicate classes within one party.

## Where should a new player begin?

Use the digital-exclusive Road to Frosthaven introduction and select a manageable difficulty. Read [getting started](/guides/getting-started/), compare [starting classes](/classes/), and explore the [world map](/maps/).
