## How do you choose what to improve?

Read the current upgrade cost, availability and effect. A future possibility is less useful than a building that resolves the party's current recurring problem. Keep material spending coordinated with equipment plans, and do not copy tabletop prerequisites without checking the digital implementation.

Consult [outpost planning](/outpost/outpost-planning/), review [equipment and crafting](/items/equipment-and-crafting/), and use the [settlement map marker](/maps/?location=frosthaven).
