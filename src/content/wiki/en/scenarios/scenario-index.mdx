## How should you prepare for a scenario?

Read the current objective and special rules before selecting a hand. Bring movement for the actual board, and preserve a flexible action when a target may die before your initiative. Do not assume the same enemy mix or reward as a tabletop page with the same title.

Open the [world map](/maps/). Only visually verified locations are plotted; these scenario numbers are not assigned invented coordinates. Prepare with [party composition](/guides/party-composition/) and [card economy](/mechanics/card-economy/).
